## Grouping decisions

- The reference homepage describes an ISA “state PPT”, but among the supplied files the only PowerPoint deck is titled **ReCircuit Campus — Innovation Pitch Deck**. It is listed under the ESE pitch-deck entry rather than inventing an absent state-PPT file.
- The “EU WEEE Rules” chip on the reference homepage does not match the uploaded rules-comparison PDF, whose actual title/content compares India, Japan, South Korea, China and Singapore. The card follows the uploaded document.
- The 5/7/2 categories mirror the reference portfolio summary; where a file itself does not explicitly state its ISA/ESE classification, the category is an organizational choice based on context and can be changed in `src/data/submissions.ts`.
- Fifteen local files become fourteen assignment cards because the poster and comic/storyboard are two deliverables for the same campus awareness campaign.
- The campus video is kept separate from the poster/comic because it is a distinct media deliverable and the course-insights document lists video and sustainability-reel activities separately.
- The tracker workbook and live dashboard sit under one state-tracker assignment.
- The two comparison files remain separate: one is a handwritten India/world generation comparison; the other is a structured comparison of rules across five Asian countries.
- The cryptography paper remains under Extras because its topic is unrelated to e-waste.
